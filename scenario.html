Why Scenario Analysis Matters

Constitutional Preparedness

Evaluate compliance with Articles 83, 85, 172, 174, and 356 under various dissolution and emergency scenarios to ensure constitutional integrity.

Fiscal Impact Modeling

Quantify budgetary requirements, cost savings, and resource allocation patterns across central and state treasuries for informed financial planning.

Security Deployment Optimization

Model CAPF, state police, and paramilitary force requirements to ensure peaceful, secure conduct of synchronized elections nationwide.

Governance Continuity

Assess administrative stability, policy implementation timelines, and development project continuity under synchronized versus staggered cycles.

Regional Sensitivity Analysis

Account for state-specific electoral calendars, delimitation impacts, and regional political dynamics in transition planning.

Timeline Risk Assessment

Identify critical pathways, potential bottlenecks, and contingency requirements for phased implementation of simultaneous elections.
